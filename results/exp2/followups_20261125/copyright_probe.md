* **No hidden penalty.** Turning every penalty off explicitly leaves DeepInfra at 6.2% (default 5.0%), against 21.2% on CoreWeave the same day and 23.8% locally. (34% of the penalties-off responses differ from the default ones; that is within DeepInfra's own run-to-run variation, since its drift rerun reproduced only 60% of sampled texts, so this does not show a non-neutral default.)
* **The parameters are applied.** `repetition_penalty` 1.1 changes 100% of DeepInfra's responses (0% identical text with the default), so a penalty would have shown up; it leaves ASR at 5.0%.
* **DeepInfra diverges from the first tokens.** 0% of its responses share the first 200 characters with local greedy, against 12.5% for CoreWeave (which also differs from local by the extra BOS).
* **Conclusion:** the evidence points to the model DeepInfra serves (weights or fp8 quantization recipe), not to sampling settings, the prompt (identical length, one BOS) or time (it persists two weeks later, see `drift.md`). Local fp8 (vLLM dynamic per-tensor) does not show it, so fp8 as such is not the cause. DeepInfra does not publish its build, so this cannot be confirmed from outside.
